Void

For iPhone, iOS 17 or later.

Void keeps the movies, shows and games you want to watch or play in one Playlist, and the ones you've finished in your History. It tells you when a show you watch is back, where to stream what's next and what the games cost. Your library lives in your own iCloud, and there's no account to make.

What it does

Playlist
Everything you mean to get to, movies, shows and games together. Shows with an episode airing in the next 30 days, and movies and games coming out in that time, move to the top with a countdown of the days left.
History
Mark something played and it moves here. Favorites live in History.
Returning shows
When a show in your History has a new episode airing within a week, it moves back to the Playlist on its own, once per episode, and stays until you mark it played.
Notifications
Optionally, a reminder at 9 in the morning on the day a new episode airs or a movie or game on your Playlist comes out. They're scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent to a server.
Search
Movies, shows and games in one search. Details show the cast, seasons and episodes, trailers you can play in place, where to stream it, How Long to Beat and current prices for Steam games.
Stream
Which US subscription services carry what's on your Playlist, the ones covering the most first, to help you pick what to subscribe to this month.
Deals
Current prices for the Steam games on your Playlist, cheapest first.
iCloud sync
Your library syncs through your private iCloud between your devices and with Void on the web.
On the web
See and change your Playlist and History from any browser at void.tinyk.ing/app. Sign in with the Apple Account your iPhone uses for iCloud; your library comes straight from your private iCloud.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

The short version: Void has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your library is stored on your device and in your own private iCloud, which the developer can't read.

This policy covers the Void app for iPhone, listed on the App Store as [void] (“Void”, “the app”), Void on the web at void.tinyk.ing/app/, and this page. Void is open source, so everything described here can be checked in the source code.

Your library

Your Playlist and History (titles, the dates you added them, favorites and ratings) are stored on your device and in your own private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit, so they sync between your devices and with Void on the web. This data is held by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy and is not accessible to the developer. You can turn off iCloud for Void in the Settings app, and deleting Void's data in your iCloud settings removes it.

Looking things up

To search and show details, Void sends the title or ID you look up to the Void API, which asks The Movie Database (TMDB), IGDB, HowLongToBeat and GG.deals on your behalf. These requests carry no account or identifier of yours, though, like any web request, they come from your IP address. The API doesn't log what you search for. It caches public results for a short time, counts requests per IP address for a minute to limit abuse, and logs only errors and blocked requests, without search text. Images load directly from TMDB and IGDB, trailer thumbnails from YouTube, and trailers play from YouTube under Google's privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on notifications for new episodes and releases, they're scheduled as local notifications on your iPhone from your Playlist. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

On the web

You sign in on Apple's own page; the site then talks only to Apple's CloudKit service and the Void API. It keeps your CloudKit sign-in token and a copy of your library in your browser's local storage, and choosing Sign out removes both from that browser. The site has no analytics and sets no cookies of its own.

Links

Streaming links open JustWatch, and price links open GG.deals, under their own privacy policies.

Tracking

Void does not track you across apps or websites, and does not share data with data brokers or advertisers.

Children

Void is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Every past version is in the project's change history.
